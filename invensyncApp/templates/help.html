InvenSync:

"Streamlining Your Inventory, Simplifying Your Success"

Welcome to InvenSync! This platform helps you manage your inventory efficiently. On the Suppliers page, you can add new suppliers, update their contact details, and delete entries as needed. The Manage page allows you to add inventory items, specify their quantity and price, and track total costs. The system also provides an inventory summary, highlighting total items, low-stock warnings, and overall inventory value. Use the provided buttons to edit or remove entries as required. Your data is stored locally for easy access.
